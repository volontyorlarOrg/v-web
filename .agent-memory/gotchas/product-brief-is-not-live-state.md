# The brief is not evidence that anything is live

The original product brief describes intended flows — Telegram sign-in, reusable
profiles and essays, applications, volunteer records, levels, reliability, an
admin panel — and none of it exists. The marketing site must never present those
as things this website offers.

The same discipline applies to facts. Only the figures and relationships in
`PRODUCT.md` and `src/lib/content/org.ts` may appear on the site: traction
numbers, the O‘ZLIDEP partnership, the three supporting institutions, the five
opportunity sources, the founding date and founders. No extra partner,
statistic, testimonial, award, office, address, or integration.

The production site holds **no sample opportunity or volunteer data at all**.
The earlier explorations used illustrative listings with an "illustrative" label;
that was the right call for a prototype and the wrong one for a public site,
where a plausible-looking fake listing is a liability whatever it is labelled.

Anything unpublished is labelled in words, not colour: the course page says it
is in preparation and that there is no schedule, enrolment, or fee.
